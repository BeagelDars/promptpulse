## 🚀 What's New in v2.0:

1. **🌟 New Ships & Pilot Skins**:
   * **`OTRITZANIE 68`**: Legendary pilot ship featuring real in-game holographic photo cockpit, plasma wings, and rainbow thruster trails.
   * **`BOBIK THE DOG`**: Cyber-canine companion featuring floppy cyber ears, glowing eyes, wagging plasma tail, and paw laser particles.
   * **`APEX DART`**, **`VOID PHANTOM`**, **`SOLAR FLARE`**, **`MATRIX GHOST`**.

2. **🌌 Shifting Dynamic Map Zones & Biomes**:
   * **Zone 1: Cyber Horizon** (0 pts) — Classic neon cyan & hot pink synthwave grid.
   * **Zone 2: Solar Inferno** (8,000 pts) — Blazing gold & crimson magma grid with solar flares.
   * **Zone 3: Toxic Matrix** (18,000 pts) — Emerald green & cyber lime digital laser corridors.
   * **Zone 4: Void Abyss** (32,000 pts) — Electric violet & deep nebula clouds with sweeping barriers.
   * **Zone 5: Quantum Overdrive** (50,000+ pts) — Chromatic prism hyper-speed corridors!
   * *Holographic warp banner announces each new zone transition.*

3. **⚡ Boost Energy Meter (Anti-Spam System)**:
   * Hyper Boost now uses a dedicated 0–100% Energy Bar.
   * Recharges by collecting Orbs (+15% per orb) and passive generation.
   * On-screen button dynamically indicates charge status (`🚀 BOOST (READY)`).

4. **🛡️ Anti-Wall Hugging Hazard System**:
   * Electrified side rails warning indicator.
   * Full-width obstacles and sweeping edge-touching barriers prevent staying on the edges indefinitely.

## 🕹️ Controls
* **Desktop:**
  * `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` — Steer Ship Left & Right
  * `Spacebar` — Trigger Hyper Boost (when Energy Bar is 100%)
* **Mobile / Touch:**
  * `◄` / `►` on-screen buttons or **drag anywhere on the screen**
  * `🚀 BOOST` on-screen action button
